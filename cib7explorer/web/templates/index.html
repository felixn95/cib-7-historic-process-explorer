What the views do

Processes and variables
Every process definition with instance counts, runtime distribution and terminations; plus the variable catalogue: which variables ever existed per definition, how widespread they are, what type they carry — without values, so the list can be handed on.
The case behind a business key
The centre of the tool: everything that happened to one business object, as a timeline including the gaps in between. Instances without a key of their own, and instances with a different key, belong to the case and are marked as such.
One process instance in detail
The sequence of activities, the user tasks, the BPMN model of the exact version that ran with the executed activities highlighted, and the variable values to open and copy.
The process landscape in numbers
Frequencies, call graph, co-occurrence, observed transitions and how many distinct sequences exist. Every number is clickable and leads to the cases behind it.
Marks
Hold on to interesting cases and instances with your own notes, exportable — without variable values.
